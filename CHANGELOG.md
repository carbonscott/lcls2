- Added `daq_sim matrix`, one command that runs the whole hardware-free matrix unattended (`psdaq.sim.matrix`, RUNBOOK §3p): one DAQ per detector type, in turn (`up --det <T>`, a recorded cycle of 1000 L1Accepts, `down`), with named assertions on its transitions, its DRP process, its counts (simxpm's L1Accepts, the TEB's recorded events, psana2's events and events with the type's data), the per-event check and its coverage evidence, and one line per type (node, job, run file, the three counts, verdicts); one run of the programs (`up --ami --pva --epics --udp --gui --pvservers` and `cycle --ami-shot`: the MEB, AMI, the system DRPs, the PyQt GUIs, the PV-server stand-ins and the GUIs on them, every check of the cycle, their evidence); then `daq_sim feature a`..`e` (c with `--ami-src`, d with `--hosts`; a feature that cannot run here, d without two hosts of the Slurm job, is NOT AVAILABLE, a failure, and starts nothing) and `daq_sim coverage`; with `--hosts A,B --locks LOCK_A,LOCK_B` it shares the types between the two hosts (B's in a step of the job there, under B's lock) and runs feature d over both.
