How to read this page

  • One fixed map keeps the same layout on every screen. Columns are stages from left to right; bands separate kinds of path. Each kind of relation has its own colour and line pattern, shown on the Emphasize buttons.
  • Three modes above the map change what it shows. Explore the parts: click or tap a part on the map, or focus it and press Enter, to open it in the card under the map. Follow one event: a tour moves one event across the same map, one step at a time. Compare kinds: small copies of the map, one for each kind of relation and one with all of them.
  • In Explore the parts, the card under the map shows the overview when no part is open: the question this page answers, a summary, and how to read the map. A part's card shows the part's detail, its inside drawn with the arrows between its pieces and tags that name the neighbours outside it (click a tag to open the neighbour), and then its description. Click any box or dashed group in the detail to describe it in the card. Design decisions, code references, sources, flows and notes for developers are folded: open a line to read it.
  • The card's head stays in view while you scroll through the card. Its arrows move to the previous or next part, in the order of the model, with the overview before the first part; Close returns the card to the overview. When a box or dashed group inside a part is open, the head gives the part's short name, then the name of that box or group. A line at the bottom marks the end of the card.
  • Emphasize buttons dim every relation except one kind and show what that kind means under the map; All shows them all again.
  • In Follow one event, use Back and Next, the numbered step buttons, or the Left and Right arrow keys while the focus is in the map section (click in it first; they do not move the tour inside a figure that scrolls sideways). The card under the map gives the step (its code references and sources folded), then a short description of the component it happens in, then the detail of its part with that component marked; "Read the full description" opens the component in Explore the parts. The card's head stays in view while you scroll through the step and has its own Back and Next; the line at the end of the card also moves to the previous or the next step. "Show the steps on" switches the figure between the map and a sequence chart of every step; click or tap a row to open that step.
  • In Compare kinds, click or tap a copy to go back to Explore the parts with its kind emphasized.
  • Boxes marked "outside repo" are mechanisms that are not implemented in this repository, such as firmware or facility services.
  • The matrix, after the line marked Reference, lists every relation between two parts in a table, by its label and a short sample of its kind's line.
  • Full size, under the map and under the sequence chart, enlarges the figure to its natural size when the page draws it smaller, so that small text is easier to read; if the figure is then wider than its frame, it scrolls sideways inside the frame, and a note next to the button says so. A figure already drawn at its natural size or larger stays as it is. Press the button again for the usual size.
  • Read as one page shows the question, the summary, every part with its sub-parts, their descriptions and the flows out of them, and then the steps of the tour, as one long document; the map, the sequence chart and the matrix are not on it. Edit this model is for the people who maintain this page.
  • The page stays where it is when you click the map, the buttons around it or the card, except for the controls that take you somewhere. "Back to the map", "Read the full description", a copy in Compare kinds and a link to a part in the card can move it, to bring the map or the card into view. Where a click on the map, the modes or the card changes the page's address, it adds no entry to the browser's history (links to other pages aside), so the browser's Back button does not undo it.
  • Links to other pages of the documentation work on the built documentation site, not when this directory is served on its own.
